# Announcement candidate — Discord and Facebook

## 5tratumOS App Store update

We have removed the legacy Umbrel-derived Global App Store source from
5tratumOS with immediate effect. Existing installed apps are not being
automatically removed, and the 5tratStore MAIN and DEV channels continue as
normal.

We are now building **5tratStore GLOBAL**: an independent third-party catalogue
created specifically for 5tratumOS. This is both a strategic product decision
and a licensing-risk decision. We want every app to have a clear original
upstream source, documented redistribution rights, original store metadata,
pinned installation components, and a recipe tested on 5tratumOS. We will not
clone or rebrand another platform's catalogue, and inclusion will never imply
that 5tratumOS developed the upstream application.

Our target is to have the replacement store framework available today, with the
first carefully reviewed apps beginning to appear today or tomorrow. That is a
target rather than a promise: legal clearance and install testing take priority
over filling the store quickly.

The first wave will focus on genuinely useful Bitcoin, Lightning, mining,
Stratum, monitoring, and infrastructure tools. Apps will be added in smaller,
reviewed batches. Each listing will also include a Feedback link so users can
report whether it works, fails to install, fails to start, or has update or
backup problems. Feedback is user-submitted; 5tratumOS will not silently send
app telemetry.

This is a short-term reduction in choice in exchange for a cleaner, safer, and
more sustainable store that we control. Thank you for bearing with us while we
do it properly.

— The 5tratumOS team

## Supporting facts for administrators (not required in the social post)

- Umbrel changed its OS repository from CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 to PolyForm
  Noncommercial 1.0.0 on 14 August 2021. This was not a recent change.
- The preceding CC licence had already been noncommercial and
  no-derivatives since 15 September 2020.
- Umbrel's official app-catalogue repository currently exposes no
  repository-level licence detected by GitHub. A public repository is not, by
  itself, a blanket right to copy and redistribute its catalogue content.
- The 5tratumOS download being free of charge is relevant context, but does not
  automatically make every use "noncommercial" under PolyForm's purpose-based
  terms.
- This announcement deliberately describes our own risk decision and does not
  allege improper conduct or motive by Umbrel.
